Contact

Open an issue on the app repository:

github.com/leon0609/dayminder-legal/issues

Use that tracker for bug reports, questions about the app, and privacy questions. Issues on GitHub are public. Do not include passwords, Apple ID credentials, payment card numbers, or journal text and photos you do not want to be public.

What helps us understand the problem

  • Lovune · 念在 version, iPhone model, and iOS version, if you can see them.
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • The wording of an error, if one appeared, without private notes attached.

Privacy

How entries, photos, and optional iCloud are handled is described in the Privacy Policy.
