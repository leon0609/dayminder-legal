Dayminder Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 25, 2026
Contact: GitHub Issues

Dayminder (“we”, “the app”) is a local-first iOS app for remembering important dates. This policy describes how information is handled in the current version of the app.

Summary

  • No account or login is required.
  • We do not operate servers that receive your dates, notes, or photos.
  • Your data stays on your iPhone, except when you use standard device backups (for example iCloud Backup).
  • We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.

Information stored on your device

Dayminder stores the following locally to provide app features:

  • Events and settings — titles, dates, categories, notes, reminders, and preferences (SwiftData).
  • Photos you attach — when you tap Add photo in Past & Memory, you choose images via the system photo picker; compressed copies and thumbnails are saved in the app sandbox.
  • Widget display data — a small JSON snapshot shared between the app and its Home Screen widget via Apple’s App Group (group.com.dayminder.app). It contains only display fields needed for countdowns (for example event title and precomputed day counts), not your full database.
  • Pro status cache — whether Dayminder Pro is active may be cached locally (UserDefaults) so the app works offline between launches.

What we do not collect

Dayminder does not collect contact info, location, health data, contacts, calendar contents, or device advertising identifiers. We do not receive payment card numbers; purchases are processed by Apple.

Permissions

  • Photos — requested only when you tap Add photo in Past & Memory, not at launch. We do not scan your entire photo library.
  • Notifications — requested only when you enable a reminder on an event. You can save events with reminders set to None without any notification prompt.
  • Calendar, Contacts, Reminders — not used in the current version.

Purchases (Dayminder Pro)

Dayminder Pro is a one-time in-app purchase (com.dayminder.app.pro) processed by Apple StoreKit. Apple handles payment and receipt validation. Restore Purchases uses Apple’s transaction APIs only. We do not store your payment details.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not share your dates, notes, or photos with third parties. The only sharing is on-device between the Dayminder app and its widget extension via App Group, as described above.

Children

Dayminder is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Questions

Please open an issue on GitHub or contact us through the support channel listed on the App Store product page.

Dayminder 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 6 月 25 日

Dayminder 是一款在设备本地运行的重要日期记录应用。本政策说明当前版本如何处理相关信息。

概要

  • 无需注册或登录。
  • 我们不运营接收您的日期、笔记或照片的服务器。
  • 数据保存在您的 iPhone 上（若您使用 iCloud 等设备备份，备份策略由 Apple 决定）。
  • 不使用第三方分析或广告 SDK，不进行跨应用跟踪。

设备上存储的信息

  • 事件与设置：标题、日期、分类、笔记、提醒等。
  • 您主动添加的照片：仅在 Past & Memory 中点击添加照片时，通过系统选择器选取；缩略图与压缩副本保存在应用沙盒。
  • 小组件显示数据：应用与主屏幕小组件通过 App Group 共享精简 JSON 快照，仅含倒计时展示所需字段。
  • Pro 状态缓存：购买状态可能在本地缓存以便离线使用。

权限

  • 照片：仅在您点击添加照片时请求，启动时不请求。
  • 通知：仅在您为事件开启提醒时请求。
  • 当前版本不使用日历、通讯录、提醒事项权限。

内购

Dayminder Pro 由 Apple StoreKit 处理支付与收据；我们不保存您的支付卡信息。
