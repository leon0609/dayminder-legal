Lovune · 念在

Important dates, kept on your iPhone

Formerly Dayminder
Developer: ANG LI (individual)

Lovune · 念在 is an iOS app for remembering important dates and keeping short personal notes with them. It is local-first: what you write stays on your device. You do not need an account.

On this site

  • Privacy Policy — how the app handles dates, notes, and photos you add.
  • Support — how to ask for help. There is no support email on this site; use GitHub Issues.

The iOS bundle identifier may still be com.dayminder.app.
